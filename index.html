Get the data

Download facts.json or facts.csv, or load them from the jsDelivr CDN so your app never calls the Cat Trivia Games site:

https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/cattriviagames/cat-facts-dataset@main/facts.json

Each fact has an id, the fact sentence, a category, the source_title and source_url of its outside source, the article_url it comes from, and the date it was added. The GitHub repository has the field notes and example scripts in JavaScript and Python.

How the facts are checked

Every fact is a sentence that a published Cat Trivia Games article states and cites. Before a fact went in, its wording was checked against that outside source, and facts the source did not support were left out. A fact about one study or one registry rule reports what that source found, and nothing here is veterinary advice. How the articles are written and corrected is in the editorial policy. To report a fact that is wrong, open an issue on GitHub with its id.
